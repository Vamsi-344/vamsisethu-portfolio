This site is my home for notes on DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering, and
platform engineering.

I work mostly around Kubernetes, OpenShift, OpenStack, bare-metal provisioning,
GitOps, CI/CD, observability, and cloud-native storage. A portfolio is useful for
showing what I have worked on, but the blog is where I want to capture the
engineering details that are easy to forget after a project ships.

The first version is intentionally simple:

- selected projects
- work history
- resume
- short technical posts

I will keep the writing practical: problems, tradeoffs, commands, architecture
notes, and lessons from building infrastructure platforms.
