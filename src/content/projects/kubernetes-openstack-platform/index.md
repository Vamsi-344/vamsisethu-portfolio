Built a highly available Kubernetes platform with three master nodes, worker
nodes, kube-vip, stacked etcd, and KubeVirt-based virtual machines. Worked on
OpenStack deployed on OpenShift and integrated APIs for VM provisioning at scale.

## Focus areas

- Highly available Kubernetes control plane
- KubeVirt and VM-as-a-Service exploration
- OpenStack on OpenShift
- API-driven VM provisioning
- Platform uptime and operability
